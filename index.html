Research Experiences

Selected ongoing, under-review, undergraduate thesis, and completed research activities.

Under Review

Revolutionizing Healthcare System Through Artificial Intelligence Implementation: A Hesitant Probabilistic Decision-Making Approach

  • Developed SWARA to do the weighting of criteria and MSD to calculate the weight of experts in a hesitant probabilistic fuzzy system to assess AI implementation strategies.
  • The combined expert-based review effectively placed the AI/IoT training and diagnostic enrichment the top for AI driven healthcare transformation.
Ongoing Research

Sizing a Sustainable Off-Grid Microgrid for River Island Electrification: The Case of Khidirpur Char

  • Optimized a grid-independent PV/Diesel/Battery hybrid renewable energy system for off-grid electrification (Khidirpur Char, Bangladesh) using HOMER Pro, NSGA-II, and MOPSO for tri-objective optimization (NPC, LCOE, CO₂).
  • Applied CRITIC–TOPSIS for weight-free compromise selection.
  • Achieved ~79% CO₂ reduction and 93% renewable fraction with zero unmet load.
Undergrad Thesis

Integrated Demand Forecasting and Inventory Management Model for Perishable Product

  • Hybrid demand forecasting model improves demand forecasting.
  • Proposed constraint awareness inventory and integrated with demand forecasting to reduce product wastages and stock outs in retails shops.

Strategic Implementation of AI and ML in the Pharmaceutical Sector: A Dual Hesitant Fuzzy Group Decision Making Approach

7th IEOM Bangladesh International Conference on Industrial Engineering and Operations Management, 2024

  • Developed a dual hesitant multi criteria group decision making framework to evaluate AI and Ml implementation strategies in the pharmaceutical sector.
  • Used the DHFHM operator to aggregate uncertain expert opinions and calculate strategy scores.

Integrating Renewable Energy and Customer Insights: A Systematic Approach to Solar Power Bank Design

7th IEOM Bangladesh International Conference on Industrial Engineering and Operations Management, 2024

  • Proposed a customer-driven solar power bank design using survey, kano model and QFD.
  • Selected optimal material using the Digital Logic Method.
  • The validated the design with CAD modeling and stress analysis which was structurally safe and feasible for manufacturing.

Comparative Analysis of Forecasting Methods and Integration with Inventory Models for Efficient Demand Management in the Electronics Sector

8th International Conference on Mechanical, Industrial and Energy Engineering 2024

  • Compared three forecasting methods and found Linear Regression most accurate.
  • Integrated forecasting with an inventory model (EOQ, safety stock, reorder level) to improve decision making.
